Press & Publishing Workflow
  • 01 Submit a Journalist / Blogger Profile Request using the form.
  • 02 The request is routed to user-management.html for manual identity verification.
  • 03 On approval, your account is upgraded to an authorised Journalist / Blogger profile.
  • 04 Draft posts with embedded images or video and click Publish.
  • 05 Posts enter the moderation queue in admin.html — nothing goes live immediately.
  • 06 Once an admin approves, the article publishes to the blog and dynamic feeds.
