Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026 — Effective: September 13, 2026

Summary: We collect only what we need to deliver penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and academy services. We never sell your data. We comply with Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and GDPR where applicable.

1. Who we are

VUNVAULT is a cybersecurity company serving SMEs, SACCOs, fintechs, and enterprises across Africa. Contact: [email protected] | Nairobi, Kenya.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact data: name, email, company, phone when you request an assessment or use contact forms.
  • Technical data: IP, browser, device, pages visited (via analytics, only with consent).
  • Assessment data: scope, scan results, reports you explicitly share for service delivery.
  • Payment data: processed securely by Paystack — we do NOT store card numbers.
  • Academy data: progress, quiz scores, certificate name stored locally + server for certificate verification.

3. How we use it

  • Deliver and improve security services
  • Communicate about your assessment, retainer, or certificate
  • Security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance
  • With consent: analytics to improve site performance, and marketing (you can opt-out anytime)

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Contract (to deliver service), Legitimate Interest (security, improvement), Consent (analytics, marketing), Legal Obligation (tax, breach notification).

5. Sharing

We share only with:

  • Payment processor (Paystack)
  • Analytics provider (Google Analytics 4, only after consent)
  • Hosting provider & email provider under DPA
  • When required by law or to protect rights/safety

We never sell personal data.

6. Retention

  • Contact inquiries: 24 months
  • Assessment reports: 36 months or per contract
  • Academy progress: until you clear browser storage, certificate records retained for verification
  • Analytics: 14 months (anonymized)

7. Your rights

Under Kenya DPA 2019 and GDPR you have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and to withdraw consent. Email [email protected] — we respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use TLS/HTTPS, encrypted backups, least-privilege access, and regular patching. No system is 100% secure — in case of breach we notify the ODPC and affected individuals as required by law.

9. Cookies

We use:

  • Essential cookies (security, consent memory) — always on
  • Analytics cookies (GA4) — only if you Accept
  • Preference cookies (language, currency) — only if you Accept

Manage via our cookie banner or your browser settings. See cookie banner for details.

10. International transfers

11. Children
