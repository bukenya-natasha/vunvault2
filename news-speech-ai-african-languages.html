Cencori and Spitch Partner to Build Speech AI Models for African Languages

Published 24 Jul 2026 Author VUNVAULT Editorial

A groundbreaking strategic alliance seeks to deploy highly localized speech recognition and natural language processing models for widely spoken indigenous African languages.

Artificial intelligence innovators Cencori and voice technology firm Spitch have announced a landmark partnership to research, train, and deploy advanced speech recognition models for indigenous African languages, addressing a major gap in the global technology space.

Unlike global language processing giants which focus primarily on English, European, or Asian languages, this localized joint venture targets widely spoken African dialects. The initial rollout will focus on training deep learning neural networks to recognize and process spoken Zulu, Xhosa, Swahili, and Yoruba, accounting for regional accents and unique local vernaculars.

Bringing the Power of Voice AI to Underserved Markets

This development is set to revolutionize voice commerce, helping banks, public utilities, and healthcare clinics across the continent provide automated, highly accessible customer support to millions of rural and non English speaking citizens. By interacting with AI assistants in their native tongues, users can resolve queries, check financial balances, or book clinical appointments with absolute ease.

Furthermore, Cencori and Spitch are prioritizing voice biometric security within these models. This ensures that automated speech systems can authenticate users accurately, safeguarding digital services against sophisticated voice cloning scams that have increasingly targeted mobile banking applications across the region.
