FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes. VUNVAULT publishes the Academy as public infrastructure. No account required. Progress is stored locally in your browser.

Is it recognised like CompTIA or a university?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. The certificate proves you completed this curriculum. Use it as evidence of initiative. Then sit the industry exams if you need a market credential.

Do I need to code?

Not for these three courses. We will not hide behind jargon. When a command or CVE appears, we explain why it matters to a human on a deadline.

Who is it for?

Anyone with a phone. Especially journalists, SACCO staff, teachers, founders, public servants, and junior analysts anywhere in the world.

Spoken audio?

Foundations includes generated spoken narration. Hygiene and First Hour are written for screen readers and silent study. We do not ship fake lecture videos.
