Vodacom Lesotho Launches Mobile Investment Solution for Wallet Holders

Published 27 Jul 2026 Author VUNVAULT Editorial

In a major step forward for financial inclusion, Lesotho mobile money subscribers can now buy government treasury bills and saving bonds directly from their digital wallets.

Vodacom Lesotho has formally announced the launch of an innovative investment product integrated directly into its mobile wallet system. This pioneering development enables mobile money users across the mountain kingdom to access and purchase government treasury bills and secure sovereign saving bonds directly from their mobile devices.

By bypassing the traditional, highly centralized banking institutions, this platform drastically reduces the barriers to retail investment for average citizens. Users can start investing with nominal amounts, receiving interest returns directly into their digital wallets on a quarterly or bi-annual basis.

This initiative represents a massive step forward in deepening financial inclusion, particularly for rural communities who historically had to travel long distances to physical bank branches in Maseru to access simple wealth building tools.

Mobile investment solutions make wealth building tools accessible to everyone.
